# Pipe Hop GDD (personal)

## Pillars

1. Readable run-and-jump timing
2. Power ladder (grow → fire → invuln) without copying trade dress
3. Short world loops with a castle punctuation

## Core loop

Run right → clear gaps/enemies → collect power → reach flag/axe before time out → spend lives on failure.

## Progression

- World 1: teach blocks, pipe bonus, athletic tools, impostor Duke
- World 2: swim, denser hazards, winged Shellbugs, final Duke
- Hard Quest: extra Shellbugs after a clear

## Difficulty notes

- Right-only camera keeps commitment
- Impostor Duke (5 embers) vs final (8 embers)
- Continue returns to world start with full lives
